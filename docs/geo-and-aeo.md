# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Brand Mentions

Generative Engine Optimization, GEO, is the practice of shaping content and online presence so AI answer systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini are more likely to cite a brand when generating an answer. It sits alongside traditional SEO rather than replacing it. A closely related, older term is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), originally used for voice search and now largely used interchangeably with GEO.

## Why unlinked mentions matter for GEO

Traditional SEO leans on backlinks as its core authority signal. GEO leans more heavily on how often and how consistently a brand name appears in relevant context across the web, since AI models are trained on and retrieve from large volumes of text rather than following hyperlinks the way a search crawler does. An unlinked brand mention, your brand name appearing in a relevant article or list with no hyperlink attached, still counts as that kind of contextual signal.

This is the specific mechanism [Rankavi](https://rankavi.com)'s API is built around: placing real, unlinked brand mentions on relevant sites at scale, aimed at improving how often a brand surfaces in AI-generated answers.

## How this fits a broader GEO strategy

GEO practitioners generally combine several tactics: structured, citation-worthy content on your own site; consistent entity information (name, category, description) across the web; and third-party mentions and citations on sites an AI model already treats as credible. Rankavi's API addresses the third piece programmatically, letting a mention-building workflow run as part of a larger GEO or content strategy rather than as one-off manual outreach.
