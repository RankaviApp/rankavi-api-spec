[Rankavi](https://rankavi.com) is a brand mention platform. We place real, unlinked mentions of your business across relevant websites, helping you get cited by name when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overviews for a recommendation in your category.

- **[GEO and AEO](geo-and-aeo)** — how unlinked mentions fit into Generative Engine Optimization
- **API Reference** — the full `POST /mentions` and `GET /categories` endpoint documentation

## Getting started

1. Create an account and get your API key from your [Rankavi dashboard](https://rankavi.com).
2. Call `GET /categories` to find the right category for your brand.
3. Call `POST /mentions` to submit your first mention order.

See the API Reference tab for full request and response details, or visit [rankavi.com/api-docs](https://app.rankavi.com/api-docs) for the complete guide.
